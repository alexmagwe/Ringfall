TASKPLAN — Round loop + escape payoff

Turning the escape into a real ending, and RUN IT BACK into a real new round.

Context

Reaching the evac pad at the maze centre currently ends in a hard cut: the character is anchored mid-stride and a static panel appears. There is no payoff beat, and pressing RUN IT BACK drops you into the same maze at the same spawn, so there is no reason to run it again.

This plan does three things:

  1. A cinematic escape. The camera unhooks and pulls up and back over the maze you just crossed, hunters converging on the pad too late, then the win panel fades in over the shot.
  2. A real new round. RUN IT BACK regenerates the maze with a new seed, so the walls change, and drops you at a new random spawn always far from the centre.
  3. A personal best, persisted through PlayerData, with a NEW BEST stamp.
touch pad → character freezes, drone fades to silence ↓ 0.0s camera unhooks from the character ↓ 1.8s rises + pulls back over the maze (Quart ease-out) ↓ 0.5s hold — hunters sprint in, too late ↓ ESCAPED panel fades in over the shot

The one thing that will bite you

MazeService rebuilds via mazeFolder:ClearAllChildren(). That destroys ExitGate and the EscapeZone part EscapeService parented in there. EscapeService captured exitGate as a local at Start() and wired exitGate.Touched once — so after a rebuild those point at destroyed instances and the win condition silently stops working.

workspace.MazeReady cannot solve this: it is set true once and never cleared, so its changed-signal will not fire again. The fix used throughout is a new monotonic counter workspace.MazeGeneration, incremented on every build including the first. Do not invent a second mechanism.

Out of scope / Do NOT

  • Do NOT give each player their own maze. A restart is a server-wide new round. Instancing is out of scope.
  • Do NOT touch SprintController.client.luau or CROUCH_ANIMATION_ID — crouch is a separate open thread.
  • Do NOT touch the duck constants in HunterService (DUCK_*, EYE_*). Eye placement was measured in Studio; do not "tidy" it.
  • Do NOT change RADII, SECTORS, BANDS, WALL_H, WALL_T, FLOOR_Y. Only the passage pattern varies per seed.
  • Do NOT clear workspace.MazeReady — services :Wait() on it and would deadlock. It stays a one-way latch.
  • Do NOT add Studio assets. Everything here is code.
  • Do NOT reassign profile.Data. Persist only via PlayerDataService.SetValue.

Steps

1 Extract a re-runnable rebuild(seed)

src/features/Maze/MazeService.server.luau

All generation is inline in Start() today. Move the body into a module-level function; Start() becomes a two-liner.

function MazeService.rebuild(seed: number)
    local rng = Random.new(seed)
    -- ... existing generation body, unchanged ...
    workspace:SetAttribute("MazeSeed", seed)
    workspace:SetAttribute("MazeGeneration",
        (workspace:GetAttribute("MazeGeneration") or 0) + 1)
end

function MazeService.Start()
    MazeService.rebuild(Constants.SEED)
    workspace:SetAttribute("MazeReady", true)
end

Account for: increment MazeGeneration last, after MazeNav.ready = true. Delete the separate unseeded spawnRng so one seed fully determines the round. Keep ClearAllChildren() — it is what makes rebuild work.

Check: lune run tools/split clean; on the server print(workspace:GetAttribute("MazeGeneration")) → 1.

2 Spawn far from the centre

src/features/Maze/MazeService.server.luau — inside rebuild

The band is currently rng:NextInteger(3, max(3, BANDS - 2)), which can land mid-maze. A run should always be a long haul inward.

local SPAWN_MIN_BAND = BANDS - 2 -- 10
local spawnBand = rng:NextInteger(SPAWN_MIN_BAND, BANDS) -- 10..12

With BANDS = 12 and RADII[13] = 534, that is roughly radius 450–534. Keep the existing sector pick, mid-radius positioning, y = 4 and lookAt toward centre.

Check: spawn distance from origin ≥ 429 on every rebuild (band mid-radii are 429, 471, 513), across 5 different seeds.

3 Re-wire EscapeService on rebuild

src/features/Maze/EscapeService.server.luau

The footgun step. Extract zone creation + gate wiring into a re-runnable bindToMaze(); move the Touched body into a named onGateTouched(hit).

bindToMaze()
workspace:GetAttributeChangedSignal("MazeGeneration"):Connect(bindToMaze)

Account for: disconnect the old Touched before reconnecting or you leak one per round. spawnLocation may stay captured — it is repositioned in place, not recreated.

Check: call MazeService.rebuild(2002), walk onto the new pad — Escaped must still fire. Before this step it will not.

4 Restart triggers a new round

src/features/Maze/EscapeService.server.luau — Net.Restart.listen 
MazeService.rebuild(Random.new(os.clock() * 1000):NextInteger(1, 2 ^ 31 - 1))

for _, plr in Players:GetPlayers() do
    resetState(plr)
    plr:SetAttribute("Checkpoint", nil)
    plr:SetAttribute("CheckpointRing", nil)
    plr:SetAttribute("SafeUntil", nil)
    local character = plr.Character
    if not character then continue end
    setCharacterAnchored(character, false)
    character:PivotTo(spawnLocation.CFrame + Vector3.new(0, 3, 0))
end

Clearing Checkpoint is required, not optional

It stores a raw world-space CFrame. After a re-carve that exact point may sit inside a new wall — and HunterService.catchPlayer teleports caught players straight to it.

Check: after RUN IT BACK the walls visibly differ, you are far from centre, timer reset, and MazeSeed differs from last round.

5 Refresh Hunter + Checkpoint on rebuild

src/features/Hunter/HunterService.server.luau · src/features/Checkpoint/CheckpointService.server.luau

Hunter snapshots cellKeys and maxBand once at Start(). Hoist both to upvalues behind a refreshNav(), call it on MazeGeneration change, and re-home every hunter via farSpawn. Checkpoint just needs table.clear(deepest) so players re-baseline.

Account for: per-hunter lastKnownCell / wanderTarget are closure locals; re-homing the model is enough, since stepToward re-reads MazeNav live and re-paths within one REPATH tick (0.7s).

Check: rebuild mid-run with hunters active — they resume within ~1s and never path through walls.

LookBackController will fight you for the camera

It writes camera.CFrame every frame at Camera.Value + 1. It bails on Caught but not on escaped. Add player:GetAttribute("Escaped") to that same early-return in LookBackController.client.luau — a one-line change.

Account for: the character is already anchored server-side, so do not anchor again. Re-check player.Character each tween step and abort to stop() if it vanishes.

Definition of Done

  1. split, check-views and check-framework-boundary all pass.
  2. Escaping plays the camera flight, then the panel appears over it.
  3. RUN IT BACK gives a visibly different layout and a spawn ≥429 studs from centre, across at least 5 consecutive rounds.
  4. The win condition still fires on rounds 2–5 — the regression Step 3 exists to prevent. Test explicitly, not just round 1.
  5. Hunters resume chasing within ~1s of a rebuild and do not path through walls.
  6. A caught player after a rebuild respawns somewhere valid, never inside a wall.
  7. Personal best survives a rejoin; NEW BEST shows only on an improvement.
  8. Camera returns to normal third person after restart, and Q still works with movement unaffected.
  9. No debug print statements remain.
