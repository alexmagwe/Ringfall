TASKPLAN — The extraction loop

Prize at the centre, exit where you started. Supersedes the deleted salvage-loop plan.

Why

The current flow has no decision in it. The goal is the hub — also the deepest, most dangerous point — so "how far do I push?" can never be asked. You always push all the way, because that's where the door is. Nothing escalates, and being caught costs nothing because a checkpoint hands your position straight back.

1. Everyone starts in a STAGING ROOM outside the maze — the extraction pad is right there, seen before you leave 2. Doors open. Descend. Salvage is worth more the deeper it spawns 3. The VAULT at the hub → taking it TRIPS THE ALARM · every hunter converges on the carrier · every player's compass swings to point at them 4. Carry it out, back through the same three sealed gates 5. VAULT REACHES THE PAD → round ends. EVERYONE banks their haul 6. Caught → haul spills, and the vault drops as its own object that ANYONE can claim

Settled architecture — do not revisit

DecisionWhy
Free-for-all; solo is the degenerate caseOne player = a race with nobody in it. No team system at all — no Teams service, no second extraction. Teams would block solo play today.
Round-based, server-wideStaging → run → extract → rebuild. MazeGeneration + the existing server-wide restart already do half.
Banked is safe, carried is at riskCash can never be lost. Haul and bought loadout are lost on death.
Permanent unlocks, per-run rentalsCash unlocks access forever; you still rent the item into each run and still lose it on death. Progression is your options widening, not your power climbing — so the FFA gap stays bounded and cash never goes worthless.
Salvage is other runners' lost kitFiction only — spilled packs, a dead runner's rig. Scattered fresh every round; nothing carries between rounds.
Compass is standard equipmentPoints at "the next thing you need" — see Phase 5.

A perfect sweep is 790, but it needs all 24 cells and the round ends the moment anyone extracts — so nobody finishes one. The vault is a single touch worth 500: it beats any realistic sweep while staying under the impossible maximum, which makes committing to it tempting rather than automatic. Purely additive phase; the game still plays as-is. Haul HUD goes bottom-right, never the top-centre stack.

Phase 2 Staging room, round lifecycle, inverted win

A walled room outside the perimeter at radius RADII[13] + 40, with one opening cut through the Perimeter wall — effectively a fourth gate, the mouth of the maze. The extraction pad lives inside it, visible before anyone leaves, which teaches the return trip wordlessly. SPAWN_MIN_BAND and the old random-band spawn become dead code; every run is now a full descent.

A RoundState attribute drives "Staging" → countdown → door opens → "Active". Only the vault carrier can end the round at the pad.

Steps 2.2 and 2.3 must land together

EscapeService.bindToMaze does WaitForChild("ExitGate"). Rename that part to Vault without rewriting the win path and bindToMaze yields forever — the round can never end, with no error.

Phase 3 The Vault and the alarm

The alarm reuses the gunshot summon — write no new hunter logic

HunterService already beelines hunters to HunterAlertPos while HunterAlert is recent. SalvageService just refreshes both at the carrier's position every second. The only hunter change: an AlarmActive flag that skips the SUMMON_RADIUS check so the whole maze responds. Leave SUMMON_RADIUS alone — the gunshot summon stays local.

Phase 4 Drop, steal, recover

In catchPlayer's task.delay(1.4) block, before the teleport: spill the haul as a recoverable pile and drop the vault as its own separate object. Two things, same spot, different meanings — touching the vault makes you the carrier and re-points the alarm at you. Neither decays; pressure comes from the maze being hot.

The hunter that caught you no longer warps away — delete that farSpawn pivot so it lingers guarding the kill. Safe: you respawn at the last gate, with SafeUntil grace.

Step 4.3 — Everything in the maze dies with the round

Nothing carries between rounds. Drops are a within-round mechanic only: if nobody recovers a pile before the round ends, it is gone. At round end, clear workspace.Drops and let the existing MazeGeneration hook re-scatter workspace.Salvage from scratch.

Do not add any cross-round store of unclaimed value. This is what keeps a loss a real loss — if value leaked forward, dying would cost you a detour rather than the haul.

Phase 6 Cash, unlocks, rentals

Do not start until 1–5 are playable — prices are unguessable until you know what a run pays. Cash and unlocks are never lost; rented kit dies with you.

Two prices per item: unlock once for permanent shelf access, then rent every round you take it in. Cheap things unlock immediately and rent for pennies; the good stuff is a real investment to unlock and still stings to take in. Renting something unowned is rejected server-side, and a player who can't afford a rent simply goes in without it — never block someone from starting a round.

The store is the one React UI here (a real screen, not a HUD readout): .ui.luau + story, dumb view, props in / intents out. Purchases validated server-side and only while RoundState == "Staging" — never trust a client-sent buy, same discipline as GunService.

Definition of Done

  1. All three lune checks pass.
  2. A round runs end-to-end with no manual button press.
  3. Salvage scatters per district, depth-scaled, at different cells every play.
  4. Taking the vault pulls every hunter and swings every compass to the carrier.
  5. Only the vault carrier can end the round at the pad.
  6. Being caught spills the haul and drops the vault for anyone to claim; the hunter lingers.
  7. Everyone banks their haul at round end, not just the winner.
  8. Nothing carries between rounds — unrecovered drops and uncollected salvage are destroyed at round end.
  9. Cash and unlocks survive a rejoin; rented kit does not survive death.
  10. Renting an unowned item, or buying outside staging, is rejected server-side.
  11. No team system, no permanent stat/power upgrades (unlocks widen options only), no global run countdown, HUD stack untouched.
  12. No debug prints remain.
